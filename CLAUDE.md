What this repo is, its layout and its hooks: see [README.md](README.md).

## Commit Message Format

Conventional Commits, PR titles too: `type(scope): lowercase sentence`, ≤128 chars; scope optional, `!` = breaking.
Types: `feat` `fix` `docs` `refactor` `perf` `test` `build` `ci` `chore`

Body optional. No AI attribution ("Claude", "AI", "agent"); human `Co-Authored-By:` trailers allowed.

## Content rule

Generic only: no product name, domain doctrine, knowledge-base pages or quoted third-party text
(CONTRIBUTING.md). A consumer's specifics go behind an extension point (README).
